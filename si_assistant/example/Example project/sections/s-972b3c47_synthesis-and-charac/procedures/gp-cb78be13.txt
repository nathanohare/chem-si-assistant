To a solution of the aryl methyl ketone (0.50 mmol, 1.0 equiv) in MeOH (2.5 mL) at 0 °C was added NaBH_{4} (28.4 mg, 0.75 mmol, 1.5 equiv) in one portion. The mixture was stirred at 0 °C for 30 min (complete conversion by TLC), then quenched with saturated aqueous NH_{4}Cl (5 mL) and extracted with EtOAc (3 × 10 mL). The combined organic layers were washed with brine (10 mL), dried over Na_{2}SO_{4}, filtered and concentrated under reduced pressure.
