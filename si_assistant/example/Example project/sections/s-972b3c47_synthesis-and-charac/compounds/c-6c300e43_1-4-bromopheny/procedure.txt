To a solution of alcohol **@{c-ad564675}** (80.4 mg, 0.40 mmol, 1.0 equiv) in CH_{2}Cl_{2} (2.0 mL) were added Et_{3}N (84 µL, 0.60 mmol, 1.5 equiv), DMAP (2.4 mg, 0.020 mmol, 5 mol %) and Ac_{2}O (57 µL, 0.60 mmol, 1.5 equiv) at room temperature. After 2 h (complete conversion by TLC) the mixture was diluted with CH_{2}Cl_{2} (10 mL), washed with 1 M HCl (5 mL), saturated aqueous NaHCO_{3} (5 mL) and brine (5 mL), dried over Na_{2}SO_{4}, filtered and concentrated under reduced pressure.
